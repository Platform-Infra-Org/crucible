# Rubrics, scoring and sign-offs

## Rubrics

The training's authors write a rubric for every question and task a person scores: what a good answer has. You see it
on the Anvil. Trainees never do. If there is none, the Anvil says so; use your judgement.

## Deciding

Give **Points**, from zero to the item's maximum, and write **Feedback**. Then:

- **Score**: the points stand. Scores are final; the trainee can't retry that item.
- **Return for rework**: the trainee gets your feedback and sends the work again. Returning needs feedback, so say
  what to change.

When a lab task's hints cost points, the Anvil tells you; that cost comes off what you award. Every decision is
recorded in the audit log, and the trainee gets an email.

## Overriding a lab task

On a lab's evidence, every checked task has **Override points**. Use it when a check got it wrong. Give a reason; the
override is audited.

## Live sign-offs

Some questions are demos the trainee shows you in person. They wait under **Live sign-offs** on the Anvil. Once you
have watched the demo, add notes if you like and choose **Mark passed**. That gives full marks. The trainee has to
have signed in to Crucible at least once.

## Resets (admins)

Scores are final, but an admin can unstick a trainee: on a quiz answer in the Anvil, or for a module on the team's
Journey page, clear the module's quiz attempts, reopen scored answers, or both. A reset needs a reason, is audited,
and the trainee is told. It never lowers anyone's forge rank.
