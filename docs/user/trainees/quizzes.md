# Quizzes

A quiz proves your temper. The page shows the **pass mark**: the share of the quiz's points you need to forge it.

## Questions Crucible scores at once

Single choice, multiple choice, an exact answer, an answer that must match a pattern, putting items in order (use the
↑ and ↓ buttons), and matching pairs. Answer them all, then choose **Submit answers**. You see which you got right
straight away, and your best attempt is the one that counts.

The author may limit the quiz:

- **Attempts**: the page shows *Attempts left*. When none are left, you can't submit again.
- **Cooldown**: after an attempt, the next one opens later. The page tells you when.

## Questions a person scores

Some questions are scored by a person on the Anvil, marked *scored by a person*:

- **Written answers**: type your answer and choose **Submit for scoring**.
- **Uploads**: attach up to five files of at most 20 MiB each, or paste an `https://` link.
- **Live sign-offs**: you show a scorer your work in person, and they mark it passed. There is nothing to submit.

While a person still has to score something, the quiz shows *Waiting on the anvil* and the item is
*Awaiting the hammer*. See [Feedback from scorers](/docs/trainees/feedback-from-scorers).

## Terminal questions

A quiz can also hold questions you answer inside the module's lab. You'll meet them there, as lab tasks.

## Final scores are final

A score a person gave you stands: you can't retry that answer. Only an answer returned to you for rework can be sent
again. If you are truly stuck, for example out of attempts, ask an admin: they can reset a quiz, and your forge rank
never drops because of it.
