# Ranks and badges

Your rank shows how much of your training you have forged, across every training you are enrolled in.

## The ranks

**Ore**, **Ingot**, **Tempered**, **Blade**, **Sword**, **Masterwork**. Each needs a higher share of your enrolled
training forged. Masterwork is all of it. Your Crucible may set the thresholds; by default they are 20%, 45%, 75% and
90%, then 100% for Masterwork.

Quizzes and labs weigh as much as their points, a reading counts for one, and a forged module counts in full.

## Ranks only rise

A rank you have earned is yours. If you are enrolled in a new training, or an admin resets a quiz, your share may
drop, but your rank does not.

When you reach a new rank, the Hearth celebrates it once, and your mentors hear about it too.

## Badges

You earn a badge for every training you finish. Badges sit under your rank card on the Hearth, with the date you
earned them.

## Yours alone

Ranks and badges are personal. Crucible has no leaderboards and never compares you with anyone else.
