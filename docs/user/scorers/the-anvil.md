# The Anvil

The Anvil is where people's work gets its score. **Anvil** shows in the top bar when you score for at least one
training. A training's editors and admins score it; its managers give those roles on
[Manage trainings](/docs/leaders/manage-trainings), often to a group such as `platform_seniors`.

## The queue

The Anvil lists everything waiting for you, oldest first:

- **Written answer** and **Upload**: answers to quiz questions a person scores.
- **Lab review**: a lab task where the trainee wrote notes on what they did.
- **Self-reported lab**: a whole laptop lab, when the training asks its scorers to look over results that were checked
  on the trainee's own machine.

Filter by training, trainee email or type, and choose **Filter**. The queue refreshes on its own.

Below it, **Live sign-offs** lists the demos trainees owe you in person; see
[Rubrics and sign-offs](/docs/scorers/rubrics-and-sign-offs).

## Opening a piece of work

Each one shows:

- the prompt the trainee answered, and the **Rubric**: what to look for;
- the answer, or the notes, links and files the trainee sent;
- **Earlier attempts**, with the feedback each one got;
- for a lab: every task with its checks and their output, the hints revealed and what they cost, and the
  **Terminal transcripts**, everything the trainee's terminals printed. A transcript is saved when its terminal
  closes.

## Who scores what

- You never score your own work.
- You never score a training you take, admins included: scoring shows the rubric, which is the answer key.
- Two scorers can open the same piece. The first decision wins; the other sees it was already decided.
