# Notifications

Crucible tells you by email when something needs you or something of yours was decided. A team can also have lab
requests, budget alerts and work waiting for a scorer posted to its Slack or Microsoft Teams channel.

## What Crucible emails about

Which of these reach you depends on your roles:

- A lab request is waiting for your approval, or was escalated to you.
- Your lab request was approved, rejected or expired.
- A budget reached 80% or its hard cap.
- Content you maintain failed to sync, or a lab scenario you maintain failed to prepare.
- A submission is waiting for your score.
- Your submission was scored or returned for rework.
- You, or one of your mentees, reached a new forge rank.
- A content edit waits for your review, or yours was decided.
- Leftover AWS lab resources need attention, or a lab is stuck while being destroyed (admins).

## Turning emails off

Open **Settings** and scroll to **Email notifications**. Untick anything you don't want. If email isn't set up on
this Crucible yet, the page says so; your choices apply once it is.

## Lab alerts in the browser

In a lab, Crucible can also warn you when the lab is about to cool down, even from another tab. Choose
**Enable alerts** on the banner the first time you open a lab, or **No thanks**.
