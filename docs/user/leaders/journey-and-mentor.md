# Journey and mentoring

## The Journey

Open your team and choose **Journey**. You see one row per person and program you may follow: the leader and seniors
see everyone, managers and scorers their programs, mentors their mentees.

Each module is a cell:

- **·** cold: not started;
- **◐** glowing: in progress, or a lab running in it;
- **●** forged: complete.

## Who may be stuck

Crucible flags a row when someone may need help:

- **May be stuck**: three or more failed checks on a lab task they haven't passed or skipped.
- **May need help**: they revealed a task's final hint.
- **Work keeps coming back**: a submission was returned for rework twice.
- **Gone quiet**: no activity for five business days.
- **Not started yet**: no activity at all.

Nobody is flagged as quiet while their work waits for a scorer: then the scorer is the one holding things up.

## Mentoring

Mentors are set on the team roster. **Mentor** shows in the top bar once you mentor someone. For each mentee you see
their rank, their heat map, anything waiting for a scorer, and their recent lab trouble. You also hear when a mentee
reaches a new rank.
