# Trainings, modules and readings

A training is a set of modules. Each module holds readings, a quiz, a lab, or a mix. Open a training from its card on
the Hearth to see them all.

## The training page

Every module lists its items with how hot each one is:

- **Cold**: not started.
- **Heating**: started, not finished.
- **Awaiting the hammer**: a scorer still has to look at your work.
- **Forged**: done.

The heat bar at the top is how much of the training you have forged. Quizzes and labs weigh as much as their points,
a reading counts for one.

## Linear or free

The training's author chooses how you move through it:

- **Linear**: a module stays locked (🔒) until you finish every module before it.
- **Free**: every module is open from the start.

## When a module is forged

Usually a module is forged when every item in it is. Some modules instead ask for a score: once your weighted score
across the module's items reaches the module's threshold, the whole module counts as forged, even if an item is
left.

## Readings

Open a reading and work through it; the bar at the top shows how far you have scrolled. When you are done, choose
**Mark as read**. That is what forges the reading.
