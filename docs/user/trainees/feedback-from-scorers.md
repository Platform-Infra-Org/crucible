# Feedback from scorers

Some of your work is scored by a person, a scorer, on the Anvil:

- written answers, uploads and live sign-offs in quizzes;
- lab tasks marked for review, where you write notes on what you did;
- whole laptop labs, when your program asks a scorer to look over self-reported results.

## Waiting

Until a scorer decides, the item shows *Awaiting the hammer* and doesn't count yet. Scorers get an email when your work
arrives, and you get one when they decide.

## Scored

A scored item shows the points and any feedback the scorer wrote. Scores are final: you can't send that answer again.

## Returned for rework

A scorer can send work back with feedback on what to change. Read it, fix your work, and send it again:
**Resubmit for scoring** in a quiz, **Submit for review** on a lab task. A self-reported lab that comes back has its
results cleared, so you redo the lab; hints you already revealed don't cost you twice.
Either way, the work goes back to the scorers.

## Who scores you

Scorers are people your team chose for the program. Nobody scores their own work, and nobody enrolled in a training
scores it.
