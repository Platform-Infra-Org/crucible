# The Hearth

The Hearth is your home in Crucible: everything you are working on, and how far you have come.

## What you see

- **Your trainings.** One card per training you are enrolled in, with its heat bar: how much of it you have forged.
  Open a card to see its modules.
- **Your rank.** Ore to Masterwork, with the next rank and how far away it is. A rank you have earned is never lost.
  Badges for the trainings you have finished sit under it.
- **Content unavailable.** If a card says so, the training's content can't be read right now. Your progress is safe;
  try again later or tell your team leader.

## Finding more trainings

**Trainings** in the top bar lists every training on this Crucible, with its estimated hours and number of modules.
Where you are already enrolled, **Open** takes you in. Ask your team leader to enrol you in the others.

## Help on any page

The **?** in the top bar, beside your name, opens the page of these docs about the screen you are on.
